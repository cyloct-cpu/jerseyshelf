# Address suggestions

Source: [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/), `admin1CodesASCII.txt` and `cities5000.zip` from https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/.

Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). `places.json` is a derived subset grouped by country and first administrative division; source IDs, coordinates and alternate-name lists are omitted. Generated by `scripts/ops/import-address-places.py`.

This is a suggestions dataset, not a shipping eligibility or postal-address verification service. Cities5000 covers places with population over 5,000 or administrative seats; smaller places and newly changed divisions may be absent. Customers can enter their own region/city. Administrative codes are GeoNames codes and must not be represented as universal ISO subdivision codes.

No customer address is sent to GeoNames. Display attribution in the checkout address section when using this dataset.
